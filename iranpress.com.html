China Reaffirms Support for Independent Palestinian State

Monday, 03 August 2026 20:45
Share Facebook Twitter Email WhatsApp

China has reiterated its unwavering support for the establishment of an independent Palestinian state, with Sun Zhen, head of the Chinese office in Palestine, reaffirming Beijing's commitment during a news conference at the Chinese Embassy in Ramallah.

Sun said China is ready to continue providing humanitarian and development assistance while working to strengthen bilateral ties with Palestine. He added that meetings with Palestinian leaders had reinforced his understanding of the hardships facing the Palestinian people.

The Chinese envoy noted that this year marks the third anniversary of the strategic partnership between China and Palestine and pledged continued efforts to deepen cooperation.
