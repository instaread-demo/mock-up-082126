Shania Twain Revisits the Violent Childhood That Shaped Her Life

Donny MeachamPublished: August 3, 2026
  • SHARE
  • TWEET

Shania Twain is opening up about one of the darkest chapters of her life.

During a candid appearance on NPR's Wild Card with Rachel Martin, the country icon revealed she grew up in what she described as a "very violent" home, where physical fights between her parents were a regular part of childhood.

"They loved each other. They went through so many ups and downs together, but they fought a lot, and they got violent often," Twain recalled.

When asked whether the violence also extended to her and her sisters, Jill and Carrie Ann, Twain didn't hesitate.

Read More: Shania Twain Reveals Secret to 15-Year Marriage With Frédéric

"Yeah, with me too. If I was standing in the wrong place at the wrong time, definitely," she said.

Twain said both of her parents "were pretty harsh physically," adding, "We definitely got smacked around a lot, and kicked around sometimes, literally kicked around."

A Violent Childhood

Although Twain said she and her sisters experienced physical abuse, she explained that her parents often inflicted the worst violence on each other.

It was very violent. My mom, you know, she would lose clumps of hair. She was bruised often in the face ... it was pretty bad sometimes. I mean, our house would shake — those walls would shake.

Looking back, Twain described those years as "traumatic," comparing the fights to "a really bad thunderstorm."

She also recalled the cycle that played out between her parents, saying her mother often tried to leave but ultimately returned.

Read More: How Shania Twain’s First No. 1 Hit Changed Everything in Country Music

"My mother did often try to leave," Twain said. "She did leave — we did leave — but he'd always find us, or she'd always give in and go back."

A Different Kind of Love

Twain has spoken openly in recent years about the importance of healthy communication in her marriage to husband Frédéric Thiébaud.

Most recently, she credited their willingness to talk through problems as one of the biggest reasons their 15-year marriage has remained strong.

10 Country Women Who Stood by Their Cheating Husbands

These country women took "Stand by Your Man" seriously. See 10 wives who remained with their cheating husbands after an affair threatened the marriage. Six of these 10 couples either remain married today, or stayed together until death truly did them part.

Gallery Credit: Billy Dukes

Jason Kempin, Getty Images

Alexis Gale and Jimmie Allen

A timeline of the ups and downs of Jimmie Allen and Alexis Gale’s relationship and marriage is lengthy. We’ll try to condense it here.

The couple married in May 2021, but that was a year after they welcomed their first child together. Publicly, things went well for about two years, until April 2023, when they announced she was pregnant again and they were separating.

Then, in May 2023, Allen was accused of sexual assault by his former manager (she filed for divorce around this time), but by July he said they were trying to work it out.

Son Cohen was born in September, and Allen’s team said the couple were still united as of Oct. 2023. The next March, he revealed he fathered twins with another woman in July 2023, giving him a total of six kids between three mothers.

By spring of 2024, it seemed to be over for good. Allen admitted he wasn’t ready to be a husband when he married Alex, and in October of that year, they divorced. There are currently no pictures of him or their children on her Instagram page.

Hulton Archive, Getty Images

Loretta Lynn and Oliver "Doolittle" Lynn

Loretta Lynn stood by husband Oliver “Doolittle” Lynn longer than just about any woman would, especially a woman with the means to support herself. Stories of his drinking, abuse and infidelity were hardly denied by the country legend. Still, she said she loved him up to the day of her death in 2022.

Doo died in 1996, and you don’t have to do much searching to find verified stories of his cheating. Her response and rationale is concerning if viewed through a modern-day lens, but perhaps more than anyone on this list, Lynn took her vows seriously.

Jason Merritt, Getty Images

Denise and Alan Jackson

In 2007, Denise Jackson wrote a book called It’s All About Him: Finding the Love of My Life in which she revealed that her husband, Alan Jackson, had an affair. The separated in 1997, but reconciled. They’ve never really shared details or indicated if the other woman was another country singer or even part of the music industry.

Rich Fury, Getty Images

Jana Kramer and Mike Caussin

Jana Kramer married Mike Caussin in 2015, but by 2016 they’d separated after she found out he was cheating on her with multiple women. He’d get treatment for a sex addiction, and soon, they’d reconcile. They even renewed their vows in December 2017.

Over the next three years, they hit rough patches, but he remained faithful and they even had another child together. Then, in April 2021, Kramer field for divorce after discovering Caussin had cheated once again. This time, it was unforgivable.

Michael Ochs Archives, Getty Images

Vivian and Johnny Cash

Johnny Cash’s first wife Vivian says the singer’s affair with June Carter Cash is what ended their marriage, but few believe this to be the only time he was unfaithful. Still, she was willing to love him through it all, until she couldn’t.

George Stroud, Hulton Archive Via Getty Images

June Carter Cash and Johnny Cash

Ironically (or perhaps tragically), Cash would end up cheating on June Carter Cash, as well. A 2013 biography by Robert Hilburn names June’s own sister Anita as one of the women the singer had an affair with. Still, the marriage lasted until her death in 2003.

Rick Diamond, Getty Images

Hannah Lee Fowler and Sam Hunt

In February 2022, Sam Hunt’s wife Hannah filed for divorce, accusing him of inappropriate marital conduct and adultery. The details of these accusations were never revealed, because she pulled the filings back a few months later. He’s alluded to some transgressions in songs released to the public — there are at least three that sound like an apology to her.

While Hunt has never admitted to cheating on Hannah before they got married, he has confessed to being a pretty bad boyfriend at times. She didn’t always stick by his side, but he was able to convince her come back after each of these arguments. The last three years have seemingly been pretty blissful for the couple, and they’re looking forward to welcoming baby No. 3 in May 2025.

Paul Natkin, Getty Images

Bettye and Ricky Van Shelton

Early ‘90s country hitmaker has been about as honest about his transgressions as you can be, but his wife Bettye really underlined what he did in a book called She Stays: How God Inspired a Friendship That Saved Bettye and Ricky Van Shelton’s Marriage.

The “Somebody Lied” singer disappeared from public life in 2006, but as far as anyone knows, he’s still married to Bettye. In fact, they’re coming up on a 40-year anniversary soon.

Frazer Harrison, Getty Images

Jessica and Jason Aldean

After Jason Aldean was caught cheating on his wife Jessica, she remained with him for eight or nine months, eventually divorcing in April 2013.

Initially he apologized for what he’d done and tried to dismiss it, but soon after the divorce he’d publicly announce a relationship with Brittany Kerr. They’ve been married since March 2015.

Jason Kempin, Getty Images

Staci and Evan Felker

2018 was a pretty wild year for Miranda Lambert. She started it in a relationship with songwriter Anderson East, but by spring was dating Turnpike Troubadours frontman Evan Felker.

He was (at the time) married to Staci Felker, and according to her version of events, he more or less ghosted her for Lambert. The Felkers would divorce that year, but plot twist: By June of 2020 they’d remarried and had a baby together.

Evan Felker found sobriety in 2019, which surely helped his marriage. It helped his band, as well: Turnpike Troubadours had more or less dissolved at that point, but are now back touring.

As for Lambert, in November 2018 she’d meet Brendan McLoughlin, a cop in NYC. A few months later, they’d get married.
